# Store-role research

Research checked on 21 September 2026 against the public role descriptions a large US beauty retailer publishes
for its store teams. They inform the work modelled in Cymbal Beauty; they do not establish access to any
retailer's internal interfaces, and every connector and record here is fictional.

| Role evidence (public role descriptions) | Implication for the workshop |
|---|---|
| The General Manager uses scheduling and task-management tools and balances forecasts, payroll, guest experience and workload. | A useful opening plan connects customer deadlines to stock, current assignments and coverage. |
| The Operations Manager owns planograms, counts, damages, testers, returns to vendors and inventory adjustments. | Inventory and loss decisions need linked operational evidence and existing work, not an isolated stock number. |
| The Experience Manager gives competency-based coaching, supports development and uses scheduling tools to manage attendance and placement. | Coverage plans need constraints; development suggestions need defined observations and available learning. |
| Task Associates use a task dashboard and store technology for shipment, replenishment, counts and merchandising work. | An associate needs their next task, product location, useful learning and a supported completion or blocker action. |

The published pickup promise is two hours or less for eligible orders placed by 6 p.m. local time. The workshop
therefore keeps individual order promises distinct rather than assigning every order the last deadline in a group.

Public descriptions establish inventory systems and physical inventory work, but do not establish universal
automated shelf sensing. Cymbal models electronic counts, reservations and locations. Fresh consistent evidence
supports a pick or replenishment; a stale count, missing location, mismatch or failed pick calls for a targeted check.

The connector contracts, fictional source records and decision rules are workshop implementation choices. Their
provenance and field meanings are recorded in the agent cards and tests.
