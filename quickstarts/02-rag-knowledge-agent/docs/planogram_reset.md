# SOP 03 — Planogram reset

Cymbal Beauty operating reference. Version 2026-09-21. This is fictional retailer training source material.
General procedures do not replace a store-specific directive, stored task deadline or recorded completion criteria.


- Resets are scheduled for Tuesday or Wednesday and must be finished before 10:00. Never start a reset on a Saturday
  or during the 10:00-12:00 traffic peak.
- Print the new planogram for the fixture. Empty the fixture into totes shelf by shelf, labelled top to bottom.
- Set shelf heights exactly as drawn, then place products left to right, faced to the front edge of the shelf.
- Every product gets its shelf label before the fixture reopens; a product without a label goes back to the backroom
  until the label is printed.
- Photograph the finished fixture and upload the photo within 24 hours; the reset is not complete until the photo is
  accepted.
- Units that do not fit the new planogram go to the backroom with a `planogram_fix` task so the count stays accurate.
