# SOP 01 — Locked fragrance case

Cymbal Beauty operating reference. Version 2026-09-21. This is fictional retailer training source material.
General procedures do not replace a store-specific directive, stored task deadline or recorded completion criteria.


Applies to every fragrance kept in the locked case (products flagged `locked_case`).
- Keys: the manager on duty holds one key and one trained key-holder associate per shift holds the other. Keys never
  leave the sales floor and are signed over in the key log at every shift change.
- Opening: open the case only for a guest who is standing with you. Take out at most two units per guest at a time and
  relock the case before you walk away, even for a moment. Testers stay outside the case.
- Case log: record every opening with the time, the product id and the number of units taken out and put back.
- Close of day: count the case against the case log. Any unit that cannot be accounted for is logged the same shift
  as an `unknown_loss` shrink event (see SOP 02) and reported to the manager on duty.
- Repeated losses: three or more `unknown_loss` events for the same product in 14 days means the manager reviews the
  case log and camera coverage with loss prevention; never question or name an associate on the sales floor.
