# SOP 04 — Promotion signage compliance

Cymbal Beauty operating reference. Version 2026-09-21. This is fictional retailer training source material.
General procedures do not replace a store-specific directive, stored task deadline or recorded completion criteria.


- Promotions run Sunday to Saturday. Put up the week's signs before the store opens on Sunday and take down expired
  signs by close on the promotion's last day.
- Every sign must match the promotion plan: product, promotional price, start date and end date. Check each sign
  against the printed proof before it goes up.
- Price mismatch found on the floor: the guest pays the lower of the sign price and the register price. Remove the
  wrong sign immediately and raise a `signage_fix` task the same day.
- A sign for a product that is not on promotion this week is removed at once and also gets a `signage_fix` task.
- Weekly audit: every Monday by 11:00 the key-holder walks every promotional sign and records the result; two
  failed audits in a row go to the district manager.
