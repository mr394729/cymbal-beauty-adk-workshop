# SOP 07 — Product comparisons and guest guidance

Cymbal Beauty operating reference. Version 2026-09-21. This is fictional retailer training source material.

- Use current catalog records for product names, prices, sizes, ingredients, fragrance-free labels and recorded skin-type descriptions. Missing attributes are unknown, not a negative claim.
- A lower-priced alternative must match the guest’s requested category and constraints using available catalog evidence. State any differences or missing attributes; similar ingredients alone do not establish equivalent performance.
- Separate the catalog’s overall rating and review count from any selected written reviews. Cite dated review IDs when describing individual experiences, and identify the sample size and selection basis.
- A review describes one person’s experience. Do not claim ingredients caused that experience, infer clinical efficacy, or guarantee suitability from a rating, ingredient list or skin-type label.
- Ask a clarifying question when a missing preference materially changes the comparison. Offer product facts rather than a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
- Current stock, reservations and pickup promises come from operational records, not this guidance. An available catalog listing does not establish store stock or available-to-promise quantity.
